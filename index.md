Studying eye gaze data has great potential to enhance both research and practice in software engineering and computing education. By examining gaze behavior, we can gain valuable insights into the cognitive processes behind programming, shedding light on the human factors that influence programming efficacy and learning.

The Fifteenth International Workshop on Eye Movements in Programming (EMIP 2027) aims to advance the methodological, theoretical and applied perspectives of eye movement research in programming, and addresses the cognitive challenges and opportunities specific to the programming domain. By bringing together experts and researchers from various disciplines, the workshop fosters interdisciplinary dialogue and new strategies for using eye tracking to improve both programming performance and educational outcomes.

EMIP 2027 is planned as a hybrid, full-day mini conference co-located with ETRA 2027. The program starts with a keynote, followed by paper presentations with feedback, a poster session for work in progress, a slot for the data correction challenge, and an interactive session such as a world café on the key issues EMIP should address. A social event closes the day.
