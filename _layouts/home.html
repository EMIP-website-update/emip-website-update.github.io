The EMIP dataset

Labs around the world ran the same code-reading experiment on loaned SMI eye trackers and pooled the recordings into one open dataset. Programmers read two short programs, Rectangle and Vehicle, in Java, Python or Scala, and answered a comprehension question about each.

Participants
269 recorded, 15 excluded
Tracker
SMI RED250 mobile, 250 Hz
Stimuli
2 programs in 3 languages
