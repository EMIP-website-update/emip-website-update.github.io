About the workshop

{{ content }}

EMIP brings together researchers who study how people read and understand code by analyzing eye movements and other behavioral data. Since 2013 the workshop has met to share methods, models, datasets and tools, and to ask what gaze behavior can tell us about the cognitive processes behind programming.
