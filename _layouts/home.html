About the workshop

{{ content }}

EMIP brings together researchers who study how people read and understand code by analyzing eye movements and other behavioral data. Since 2013 the workshop has met to share methods, models, datasets and tools, and to ask what gaze behavior can tell us about the cognitive processes behind programming.

The EMIP dataset

Labs around the world ran the same code-reading experiment and pooled their recordings into one open dataset: 269 programmers recorded (15 excluded), reading Rectangle and Vehicle in Java, Python or Scala on an SMI RED250 tracker at 250 Hz. Free to use under CC BY 4.0.
