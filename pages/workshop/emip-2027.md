The workshop day, call for papers and submission details will be posted on this page. For the scope of past calls, see the [EMIP 2026 call for papers](/workshop/emip-2026/).

# About the workshop

The study of eye gaze data has great potential for research in computer programming, computing education, and software engineering practice. EMIP focuses on advancing the methodological, theoretical, and applied aspects of eye movements in programming. What can gaze behavior tell us about cognitive processes during programming? This question helps us understand the human factors involved in programming.
